Have you ever imagined a day without your phone? No Google Maps, no TikTok, no GoFood, no ChatGPT. Feels impossible, right? That's because informatics has become the air we breathe — invisible, but we can't live without it.

"Informatics is not just about computers. It's about how humans, data, and machines work together to solve real problems."

1 What is Informatics?

Informatics is the science of information — how we collect it, store it, process it, and use it to make decisions. If Computer Science is about building the machine, Informatics is about what the machine does for humans.

💻

Simple Definition

Informatics = People + Data + Technology. Three things that must work together.

2 Informatics in Daily Life

You meet informatics from morning to night. Your alarm, your ojek online, your Spotify playlist, your TikTok FYP — all of them are informatics.

📱

Example in Your Day

07:00 Alarm • 08:00 Gojek • 12:00 GoFood • 19:00 Netflix • 22:00 TikTok. All informatics!

3 Data is The New Gold

Every click you make creates data. Companies use that data to know what you like, what you need, and what you will buy next. Whoever controls data, controls the future.

# Data is power
user_data = collect(clicks, likes, searches)
insight = model.analyze(user_data)
→ recommendation = personalized for YOU

4 Artificial Intelligence

AI is the brain of informatics. It learns from data, just like you learn from experience. ChatGPT, face unlock, Google Translate — all AI.

🤖

AI is Not Magic, It's Math

Behind every smart AI, there are thousands of math formulas and millions of data. AI doesn't think like humans, it calculates patterns.

5 Cybersecurity

The more we live online, the more we need protection. Cybersecurity is the bodyguard of informatics — protecting your password, your photos, your money.

🔒

Stay Safe

Use strong passwords, turn on 2FA, don't click random links. Your data is your responsibility too.

6 Cloud & Big Data

Where do your 10,000 photos go? To the Cloud. Cloud computing lets us store unlimited data without needing a huge hard disk. Big Data lets us analyze millions of data in seconds.

7 The Dark Side

But informatics can be dangerous too. Fake news spreads in seconds, our data can be stolen, and we become addicted to screens. We must be smart AND wise users.

⚠️

3 Dangers to Watch

1. Hoax & Fake News • 2. Data Theft & Scam • 3. Screen Addiction. Always check the source before you share.

8 Future of Informatics

What next? Quantum computers, smart cities, self-driving cars, doctors using AI to detect cancer earlier. Informatics in 2030 will be even more invisible — and even more powerful.

★ Conclusion

Informatics has changed the way we live, learn, and work. The question is no longer "Do we need informatics?" The real question is, "How will we use it to make our life better?" The answer is in your hands.
